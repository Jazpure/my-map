Sometimes a memory is clear, a fixed and salient point in a place, a feeling or even in the presence of another person. I like to think of these memories as coins in a fountain catching the light perfectly, inviting you to explore. Regardless of their valence, negative or positive, they are probably my most cherished possessions but if I was to point to some sort of history that could explain my inner life it wouldn't be the coins in the water it would be the other experience of memory, one that we all share. An experience that spans far and rests upon me and you in the softest way, just as the horizon hides its curve over space, I often miss its words as they are spread into a hum. This area of our memory is defined by partially forgetting, overcrowding, confusing details, and just the general patina of time. It's an area that I've found myself, recently, much more than usual. And while its not as immediately useful as the declarative region of myself it is the part that is most me.
