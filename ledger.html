1 · Geometry

2026-08-12 – 2026-08-14

№TypeDecision

2 · Sound

2026-08-18 – 2026-08-26

№TypeDecision
2.1directionDensity, not location. One shared pool of seventeen recordings; sparse ground raises distant rumble, dense ground raises crowds. Nothing switches on or off.
2.2directionA fluid whole, not components. All clips sound at all times; moving alters balance only. Overview is a filtered distant wash, never silence.
2.3meansEvery parameter is a curve over one scalar per layer — its proximity to the camera.
2.4meansConstant power: slot levels normalised so the sum of squares is fixed. A rise in one clip is exactly a fall in another.
2.5fixZoom→altitude linear, so anchored material died within one zoom level of the ground. Made logarithmic.
2.6fixMeasured loudness ranked a quiet night above a dense crowd — library masters are level-matched by the publisher. Filename descriptors at 0.72 weight instead.
2.7fixThree defects: per-grain random pan; 45 % duty cycle; rotation as fade-out, four-second wait, fade-in. Rebuilt on overlap-add — triangular envelopes at half-overlap sum flat — fixed hop, one drifting pan per voice, rotation only while inaudible.
2.8directionNo synthesised material. A layer with no recording is silent rather than invented.

4 · Typography

2026-09-08

№TypeDecision
4.1directionText is the substance. Building outlines become writing, obscured in proportion to visit density — density makes illegibility, not emphasis.
4.2directionEnglish, Courier, primary inks. One colour per glyph. A braille scheme was proposed and rejected.
4.3directionWhite ground. Printed, not emissive — which invalidates the additive colour model of 4.2.
4.4directionNo colour interaction. Overlaps do not mix; density shows as crowding alone.
4.5directionScatter far, gather close, atomise closer. Up to 1900 m of displacement at the widest view, resolving to true position, then breaking into particles.
4.6directionDepth by size. Glyph size varies with distance from the cluster centre; draw order sorted to agree with it.
4.7directionProse field. The essay set across the boroughs and deformed by visit density — displaced, rotated, jittered, and inked from pink toward the glyph colours.
4.8meansScanline typesetting: each line of type intersected with the boundary edges, crossings sorted, glyphs set only in interior spans. 132,092 glyphs — avoids as many point-in-polygon tests.
4.9fixMultiply blending read black: deck.gl draws to its own transparent canvas. Rendered interleaved into the MapLibre framebuffer.
4.10fixDeformation field at 170 m / 5 passes left 97 % of the page flat. Widened to 320 m / 7 passes, response exponent below unity.
4.11fixProse sized in metres was unbounded on approach — 66 px against 10 px glyphs, inverting the hierarchy. Capped at body size.

6 · Hand type and birds

2026-09-09 – 2026-09-10

№TypeDecision
6.1directionHalf the alphabet is drawn by hand. A Courier specimen drawn in marker replaces 40 % of the glyphs at random. Colour, size and position unchanged — the only difference is the letterform.
6.2meansSegmented by connected components; rows clustered from every mark, since ? and ! rise above the line their dots sit on. d, q and x were ringed placeholders — found by bounding-box containment, built by reflection from b, p, X.
6.3meansOne scale per set from the set's own median reference — cap height for capitals, x-height for lowercase — against per-row baselines. Ascenders and descenders keep their drawn length. 58 glyphs.
6.4fixGreyscale atlas composited its black ground as opaque and every glyph drew as a filled square. The icon layer reads alpha; sheet rewritten as RGBA.
6.5directionHand-drawn birds cross the page. Flocks of one to three on arcs that never cross the centre of the frame, in screen space, each flock fixed at an altitude so the view can pass it.
6.6directionThe beak leads. Birds enter from outside the frame rather than appearing in it, and grow rather than vanish as the view comes down.
6.7directionBirds are opaque to each other. A flock may be faint, but within it nothing shows through; the higher flock takes precedence.
6.8meansEleven drawings, hand-set regions; ruled edges rejected by a straightness test on their principal axes; silhouettes closed by dilate, flood, erode. Each flock draws into a buffer at full strength and composites once, descending altitude last.
6.9meansA quadratic with both ends off-page, its control point placed so the curve's midpoint sits at the requested clearance — the centre stays clear without testing the curve.
6.10fixFrame-time capped at 0.05 s held flight to a third of its set speed below 20 fps, which is where the page runs on integrated graphics.

7 · Connection, repair, the close view

2026-09-10

№TypeDecision
7.1directionTrain lines join the uptown days to downtown. The near-lit rule had dropped exactly the track that does the connecting, leaving Inwood and East Harlem as islands.
7.2meansGraph on shared node ids; shortest run of real track kept whole. Both ends seeded from every platform within 900 m of the trace — the IRT and IND are separate networks, so one boarding point decides the system before the search runs. Lexington Avenue and Broadway–Seventh Avenue; 19.6 km, gaps only.
7.3fixThree routes to permanent silence: listeners disarmed after one attempt, the started flag set before the graph existed, the bus gated on all four clips. Every gesture is now another attempt.
7.4directionSomething is always flying at the far and middle views. Flocks are born at the altitude the camera is at; the opening view had been empty three fifths of the time.
7.5directionBirds stop before the close view. Thinning from zoom 13.1 and gone by 14.3 — two zoom levels short of where the letters atomise. Rarer and fainter together, so they wean off rather than switch off.
7.6directionThree-quarter birds withdrawn. Seen from the side, they put the viewer level with the bird — a different vantage from the one the map is drawn from.
7.7directionBirds smaller and fainter on average as the city comes up. The mean moves, the spread does not, so a close view still throws up a large one.
7.8fixThe wingbeat was applied in each sprite's own frame, so birds drawn pointing up were stretched along their line of flight. Now applied across the heading.
7.9directionDesign note, bottom left, fading out between zoom 11.0 and 12.3 — gone ahead of the birds, being the one thing on the page that is not the work.
